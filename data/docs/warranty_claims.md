# Warranty Claims SOP

## Coverage
- Battery warranty: 8 years or 80,000 km, whichever comes first.
- Vehicle warranty: 3 years or 30,000 km, whichever comes first.
- Not covered: tyres, brake pads and bulbs, because they are wear items.

## Filing a claim
- A claim must be filed within 14 days of discovering the fault.
- Required documents: purchase invoice, service history and photos of the fault.
- Claims are filed through the dealer portal.

## Approval
- The Warranty Desk gives a decision within 5 working days.
- Claims above ₹50,000 need approval from the Regional Manager.
