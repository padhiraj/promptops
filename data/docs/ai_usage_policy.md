# AI Tool Usage Policy

## Approved use
- Only the internal AI assistant may be used with company data.
- Never paste customer personal data, such as names, phone numbers or addresses, into any AI tool.

## Review and labelling
- Customer-facing AI output must be reviewed by a human before it is sent.
- AI-generated content in external documents must be labelled "AI-assisted".

## Reporting and maintenance
- Report wrong or unsafe AI outputs with form AI-FB-01 within 2 working days.
- The AI Enablement team reviews the prompt library every month.
