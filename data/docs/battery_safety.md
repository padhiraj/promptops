# Battery Handling and Safety SOP

## Storage
- Store battery packs at 15°C to 30°C with humidity of at most 70%.
- A storage bay holds a maximum of 20 packs.
- Store packs at a 40% to 60% state of charge.

## Damaged packs
- Move a damaged pack to the quarantine cage immediately.
- Report the damaged pack to the Safety Officer within 1 hour.

## Working on packs
- Lockout-tagout must be completed before any pack is disconnected.
- Only Level 2 certified technicians may disconnect or open a pack.
- Wear insulated gloves rated for 1000 V and a face shield.
- Never bypass the safety interlock.
