# Spare Parts Returns and Refunds SOP

## Eligibility
- Parts can be returned within 30 days of purchase if unused and in original packaging.
- A return authorisation (RMA) number is required for every return.
- Helmets cannot be returned once used.

## Fees and refunds
- A restocking fee of 10% applies to non-defective returns.
- Refunds are processed within 7 working days to the original payment method.

## Defective parts
- Defective parts are replaced free of charge within 48 hours at the dealer.
- The company pays return shipping for defective parts.
