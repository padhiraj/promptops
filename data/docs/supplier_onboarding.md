# Supplier Onboarding SOP

## Registration and compliance
- New suppliers submit registration form SUP-01.
- Compliance check: a valid ISO 9001 certificate and GST registration are mandatory.
- Financial review: the supplier's credit score must be at least 650.

## Audit and trial period
- A site audit is carried out within 30 days of registration approval.
- During the first 90 days, trial orders are limited to ₹5,00,000 in total.

## Approval and payment
- The Procurement Head gives final approval.
- Standard payment terms are net 45 days.
- Suppliers are reviewed every quarter using a scorecard.
