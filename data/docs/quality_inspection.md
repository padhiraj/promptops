# Incoming Quality Inspection SOP

## Sampling and acceptance
- Inspect a sample of 10% of each lot, with a minimum of 5 units.
- A lot is rejected if the defect rate is above 2%.

## Non-conformance handling
- Raise a non-conformance report (NCR) within 24 hours of finding a defect.
- The supplier must reply with an 8D report within 7 working days.

## Records and equipment
- Inspection records are kept for 5 years.
- Measuring gauges are calibrated every 6 months.
